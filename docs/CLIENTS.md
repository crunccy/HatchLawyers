# Client roster

Fictional, kid-friendly characters (no real people — see [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) "MVP Risk Fixes" §6). Models: `Models - Characters/Clients/`
(built by `tools/clients.py`; ids are referenced in Config, never rename one). Tier = client rarity tier in
`Config.Clients` (Common ×1, Uncommon ×3, Rare ×8 payout).

## Common
**Neighbor Dave** (`NeighborDave`) — The king of dad jokes. Mows his lawn at 6 a.m. sharp, wears socks with
sandals on principle, and has never once lost an argument about barbecue.
*Case:* Got a parking ticket… for parking in his own driveway.

**Grandma Pearl** (`GrandmaPearl`) — Sweet as pie, sharp as a tack. Bakes cookies for the whole street and
drives her mobility scooter like it's a race car. Her pearls were a gift from "a very handsome sailor."
*Case:* Caught going 4 mph over the limit in the park. She calls it "a misunderstanding about momentum."

**Skater Kev** (`SkaterKev`) — Chill teen who is always "just practicing a trick." Has a sticker on everything
he owns, including his little brother.
*Case:* Accused of scuffing the town's founder statue mid-kickflip. He swears the statue moved first.

## Uncommon
**Rosa the Baker** (`RosaTheBaker`) — Proud, loud, and makes the best croissants in town. Wields her rolling pin
like a gavel and believes every problem can be solved with fresh bread.
*Case:* Ordered 100 bags of flour, got 10 — and the supplier says "that's basically 100."

**Captain Barnacle** (`CaptainBarnacle`) — Retired sea captain who tells every story twice and claims to have
arm-wrestled a squid. Lives in a lighthouse that is mostly fishing nets.
*Case:* In a bitter feud with a seagull over who owns "his" fishing spot on the pier.

**Dr. Fizz** (`DrFizz`) — Excitable inventor; has singed his eyebrows off twice this year. Every invention is
"perfectly safe in theory."
*Case:* His prize-winning soda volcano covered the entire science fair in green goo.

## Rare
**Tung Tung Sahur** (`TungTungSahur`) — A living log with a baseball bat and a very loud wake-up routine. Nobody
knows where he came from, only that he is always awake at midnight.
*Case:* Property damage after a midnight "rampage" he insists was just enthusiastic drumming.

**Baron von Banana** (`BaronVonBanana`) — A posh monkey tycoon with a monocle, a top hat and a banana empire.
Refers to himself in the third person and tips in banana peels.
*Case:* Accused of buying up every banana in town "to keep them safe."

**B0-P "Beep"** (`BeepBot`) — A cheerful delivery robot who means extremely well. Speaks only in happy beeps and
has a 100% delivery rate, technically.
*Case:* Delivered 300 pizzas… to the wrong planet.
