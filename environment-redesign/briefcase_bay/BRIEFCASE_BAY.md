# World 1 — Briefcase Bay (Stage 5, 2026-10-18) · APPROVED by crnuch 2026-10-18

A bright business and legal district on its own floating island: blue-glass towers, office parks, a harbour, and the
boss's boardroom plaza. Built in the shared kit (`../kit/hl_kit.py` + `hl_island.py`), in the hub's style.
```bash
Blender -b --python environment-redesign/briefcase_bay/bb_build.py              # .blend + layout JSON + renders
Blender -b --python environment-redesign/briefcase_bay/bb_ambience.py -- clips  # + ambience, MP4 previews
```
`-- norender`, `-- only=aerial,layout` and `-- stills` work as in the hub.

## Roblox compatibility notes (integration is a later, approved stage)
**Dynamic, so not scenery:**
- eggs on `EggSpots` (ZoneService); the boss (BossService + Bosses.client); the arrival spot (`Arrival`)
- the RETURN HOME portal's travel-while-carrying rule: a code change for later (crnuch's decision 4)
- `Boss_SeniorPartner` in the .blend is only a scale reference

**The boss needs a new `Bounds`** for this island (above). `Config.Zones.List` keeps the zone id `BriefcaseBay`
(License 0).

**Collision:**
- walkable: ground, plazas, streets, parks, arena, stairs
- no collision: canopies, flowers, signs' faces, water, papers, clouds
- invisible boxes for buildings and cliffs

**Streaming:** each building, park and the arena are their own objects.

## Known weaknesses (honest)
- **Faceless towers:** the towers read as stylised glass boxes from the ground (no interiors behind the glass).
- **Plain ferry:** the Bay Ferry is simple.
- **Untouched south lawns:** the south lawns are only groves and hills.
- **Reference check:** compared by eye to crnuch's reference thumbnail (a dense blue skyline). Ours is less dense
  by design: 12 buildings, so the parks and the arena stay readable.
