## The studded-brick kit (`kit/hl_kit.py`)
- **Bricks:** chamfered boxes built directly as geometry (0.08-stud bevel), so edges catch light like toy bricks.
  Plastic shading (roughness 0.42).
- **Raised studs:** Roblox's own rounded-square stud (from `tools/blender/stud_tiles.py`, 48 tris). Every studded
  top gets a 1-stud grid of studs, stored as a point cloud per colour and drawn with **Geometry Nodes Instance on
  Points**: one shared stud mesh (linked instances), never modelled one by one.
  - The hub has **681,024 studs**.
  - Studs skip tiny parts (< 1 stud) and anything covered (roads, lots, ponds, buildings).
- **Pieces:**
  - roofs: stepped studded gable / hip
  - upright rings and discs (portal, gears, round windows)
  - voxel domes and eggs (only their exposed rings get studs)
  - windows (frame, mullions, sill, shutters), doors (panels, arch, knob), columns, stairs, railings
  - lamps, benches, planters, flower beds, hedges, flags, banners
  - 4 tree types (round, pine, palm, blossom), bushes, rocks
  - attraction signs (black + gold + bulbs) and wayfinding signs (wood + white), per CLAUDE.md
- **Lighting:** EEVEE with ray-traced shadows/AO, a gradient sky, a warm sun, compositor bloom on glowing parts,
  Standard view transform (keeps the palette's saturated toy colours).

## Roblox compatibility notes (integration is a later, approved stage)
- **Scale:** 1 stud = 1 m, world-aligned. Hub centre = (0, 0, 0); north = −Z.
- **Positions:** every service and lot position is in `hub_layout_detailed.json` (Roblox x/z).
- **Dynamic vs permanent:** `Hub_Firms_DYNAMIC` is never shipped as scenery: FirmBuilder builds the firms. The
  lots ship as foundations; Plot `Base` parts must stay 56 deep at the street edge (`FirmBuilder.frame`, see the
  audit).
- **Studs:** in Roblox, raised studs are the client stud layer (`StarterPlayerScripts.Studs`, tag `StudTop`, uploaded
  tiles) near the camera, with `TopSurface = Studs` far away. They aren't baked into meshes, so they cost nothing
  far away and nothing on the server.
- **Triangle budget (excluding studs):** 863k.
  - Cliffs are the heaviest at 375k: before export, merge each tier's cladding per colour into a few MeshParts and
    drop hidden faces, or rebuild the far cliffs as one low-poly MeshPart per tier.
  - Vegetation is 133k: trees become 4–5 reusable MeshParts (one per type) placed as instances.
- **Collision:** walkable = ground, plaza, roads, lots, stairs, platforms; decorations get `CanCollide = false`
  (trees' canopies, flowers, signs' faces, water, clouds); cliffs = a few big invisible box/convex parts.
- **Materials** (`hl_kit.ROBLOX_MATERIAL`):
  - plastic looks → Plastic in the CLAUDE.md palette
  - `glow_*` → Neon, `glass*` → Glass (Transparency ~0.4), `water` → Glass or Terrain water
  - metal / gold → Metal or gold-coloured Plastic
  - **Blender-only:** bloom (Roblox has Bloom in `Lighting`), translucent sorting, the emissive portal field
    (Neon + transparency in Roblox)
- **StreamingEnabled / mobile:**
  - each firm lot, garden wedge and cliff tier is its own object, so they stream in pieces
  - lamps' lights, the portal field and waterfalls are cheap Neon
  - clouds and islets are low-poly and decorative only

- **Firm look:** the example firms follow the game's real stages, but the in-game firms are generated by
  `FirmStages`. A matching restyle of those fronts would be a separate, gameplay-side task.
- **Arcade Pier:** a placeholder for mr.robe's booth layout (active claim).
- **Waterfalls:** static (animation comes in Stage 4).
